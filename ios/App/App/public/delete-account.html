1. Data Deletion Policy

In full accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global data protection regulations (GDPR & CCPA), users of Oblivion 1 Fitness Club have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of their athlete account and all associated personal records.

Upon processing your deletion request, the following data is permanently purged:

  • Athlete Profile: Account identifiers, email address, profile handle, and preferences.
  • Workout Logs & History: All recorded exercise sets, weights, repetitions, RPE ratings, and program logs.
  • Telemetry & Biometrics: Historical step logs, active caloric burn estimates, cardio routes, and volume telemetry.
  • Nutrition & Fuel Data: Daily macro records, logged meals, and caloric goals.
  • Cloud Backups & Sync: All synchronized cloud documents and associated telemetry stores.

Warning: Deletion is irreversible. Once completed, your past athletic milestones, personal records (PRs), and telemetry archives cannot be recovered.

2. Option A: Instant In-App Reset

If you have access to the mobile application, you can immediately erase your locally stored telemetry data:

  1. Open the Oblivion 1 Fitness Club app on your device.
  2. Navigate to Log / Telemetry or application Settings.
  3. Select Clear Local Telemetry & Caches.
  4. Confirm the reset prompt to wipe all local workout and nutritional state.

3. Option B: Request Complete Account Erasure

To request permanent server-side erasure of your athlete account and all cloud telemetry, submit the form below or email our support team directly.
