Privacy Policy

Oblivion 1 Fitness Club (O1FC) • Effective Date: September 20, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

At Oblivion 1 Fitness Club ("O1FC", "we", "our", or "us"), your personal privacy and fitness telemetry security are fundamental priorities. This Privacy Policy discloses how our mobile and web applications collect, process, store, and safeguard your training data, biometrics, and telemetry.

We operate under a strict Zero Third-Party Sales principle. Your health, workout, and nutritional telemetry belong exclusively to you.

2. Information We Collect

To provide high-performance workout programming and athletic telemetry, O1FC processes the following categories of data:

  • Workout & Training Logs: Exercises performed, set counts, repetitions, target loads (kg), rate of perceived exertion (RPE), rest intervals, and volume calculations.
  • Activity & Sensor Telemetry: Daily step counts, estimated active caloric expenditure, cardio distance estimates, and workout timestamps.
  • Nutrition & Fuel Logs: Caloric goals, logged dietary items, meal timestamps, and macronutrient breakdowns.
  • Athlete Profile & Preferences: Chosen athlete handle/name, training preferences, muscle split targets, and interface themes.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to power the core functionalities of the O1FC application:

  • Calculating microcycle load, weekly volume distribution, and recovery energy readiness.
  • Operating the tactical rest clock and exercise cadence tracking.
  • Displaying historical training audits and athletic telemetry reports.
  • Personalizing adaptive session recommendations through the Intel Coach module.

4. Data Storage & Security

O1FC utilizes a secure, local-first storage architecture backed by authenticated encrypted cloud sync where applicable. All network transmissions use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

Local database caches are stored strictly within the sandboxed application container on your mobile device.

5. Strict No Third-Party Sales Guarantee

We DO NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, biometric estimates, or workout telemetry to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or insurance companies.

We do not inject third-party behavioral tracking SDKs or ad networks into the application runtime.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You retain complete ownership and sovereignty over your fitness data. You can delete individual workout logs, reset local caches, or request complete, permanent erasure of your account and all associated telemetry at any time.
